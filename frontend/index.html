Clinical Nasal Morphology Library

Explore the four distinct clinical nasal morphologies supported by the 2D deformation engine. Selecting any morphology pre-configures its displacement vectors and opens the Deformation Studio.

1. Saddle Nose
Cartilaginous dorsum collapse / depressed bridge
Saddle Nose

Characterized by a sunken or depressed nasal dorsum caused by loss of cartilaginous support, presenting as a scooped profile.

  • Depressed mid-nasal profile and reduced tip projection
  • Scooped dorsal aesthetic line on profile examination
  • Viewpoints most affected: Left 40°/60° and Right 40°/60°
  • Algorithm: Inward displacement along nasal dorsum normal vectors
Profile Views · Default: 0.45
2. Hump Nose
Osteocartilaginous dorsal prominence / kyphosis
Hump Nose

Prominent dorsal convex bump along the osteocartilaginous junction, a primary indication in aesthetic and corrective rhinoplasty.

  • Convex protrusion along the upper lateral cartilage and nasal bones
  • Elevated dorsal height above the ideal aesthetic profile plane
  • Viewpoints most affected: Left 40°/60° and Right 40°/60°
  • Algorithm: Outward displacement and convex curvature warping
Profile Views · Default: 0.40
3. Broad Nose
Pachyrrhine vault / widened alar base
Broad Nose

Characterized by expanded bony width and increased alar base intercanthal distance across ethnic APAC facial variations.

  • Widened alar base flare and flattened dorsal aesthetic definition
  • Expanded inter-alar distance on frontal and basal inspection
  • Viewpoints most affected: Frontal (0°) and Basal
  • Algorithm: Bilateral symmetric lateral expansion displacement field
Frontal & Basal · Default: 0.50
4. Deviated Nose
Septal deviation / asymmetric nasal axis
Deviated Nose

Asymmetric lateral deviation of the nasal pyramid or septum off the vertical midline, manifesting as C-shaped or linear tilt.

  • Lateral displacement of the dorsal aesthetic line and tip apex
  • Asymmetric nostril apertures and columellar obliquity
  • Viewpoints most affected: Frontal (0°) and Basal
  • Algorithm: Directional lateral shear displacement (Left / Right)
Directional L/R Control · Default: 0.40
